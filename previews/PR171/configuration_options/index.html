Configuration options

All configuration arguments accepted in YAML configuration files, with their types and default behavior, generated from config/default_configs/default_config.yml. See Creating custom configurations for how to use them.

passive_tracersNothingInert tracers released in small boxes above the tropopause and removed below it, to measure how long air stays in the stratosphere [`\~` (default, off)]. A map with exactly one of `release_grid` (a regular latitude by height grid) or `release_boxes` (an explicit list of boxes), plus optional `heights_from` (`tropopause` or `altitude`), `production_rate`, `loss_timescale` and `tropopause`. See docs/src/passive_tracers.md.
water_tracersNothingTracers that split total water `ρq_tot` by where it entered, adding one prognostic field `ρq_tag_\<name\>` per entry [`\~` (default, off)]. A list of entries, each with a unique `name` and a `region`, a `source`, or both. Requires `microphysics_model: 0M` or `1M`. See docs/src/tracer_configuration.md and docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_updraft_copyBoolExperimental. Whether the `water_tracers` have a copy in each updraft under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` [`false` (default), `true`]. `false` moves each tag by its share of the parent's sub-grid mass flux of water plus an exchange of composition at the updraft's mass flux, and `true` is a comparison mode that gives each tag a tracer `q_tag_\<name\>` in the updraft, moved as any other updraft tracer. `true` is refused without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_transportNothingHow the `water_tracers` follow the parent's implicit terms [`tracer`, `increment`, `\~` (default)]. `tracer` moves each tag as a tracer, and `increment` skips the tags' explicit vertical advection and after each Newton solve moves the difference between the parent's increment of `ρq_tot` and the tags' as a vertical flux. `increment` needs region tags without sources that partition the domain, an algorithm that solves every stage it uses such as the default ARS343, and an `energy_q_tot_upwinding` other than `none`, and `\~` chooses it under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` where those hold, without updraft copies and not with 1M stepped explicitly, where it is opt-in. `increment` is refused without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_precipitationBoolExperimental. Whether each of the `water_tracers` is split into the water that is neither rain nor snow, rain and snow [`false` (default), `true`]. The parts are the fields `ρq_tag_\<name\>`, `ρq_rtag_\<name\>` and `ρq_stag_\<name\>`, each a share of its own part of the parent. It needs `microphysics_model: 1M`, is refused under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` or `edonly_edmfx` and with `water_tag_updraft_copy: true`, and a restart must keep the key as the checkpoint has it. `true` is refused without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water_precipitation.md.
water_tag_precipitation_auditBoolExperimental. Whether the rain and snow parts of `water_tag_precipitation` keep the microphysics audit [`true` (default), `false`]. `true` adds two state fields per tag, `q_rtag_aud_\<name\>` and `q_stag_aud_\<name\>`: what the net-flow rule would have given the tag's rain and snow parts, less what the gross flows gave. `false` leaves them and their kernels out. The parts and the model's fields are the same either way, and a restart must keep the key as the checkpoint has it. `false` is refused without `water_tag_precipitation: true`. See docs/src/tagged_water_precipitation.md.
water_tag_ledger_per_tagBoolExperimental. Whether each of the `water_tracers` keeps its own state ledgers of what the corrections changed it by [`false` (default), `true`]. `true` adds `q_tag_led_fix_\<name\>`, the limiters' rescale and the partition repair, and under `water_tag_transport: increment` also `q_tag_led_inc_\<name\>`, what the correction after each solve moved into or out of the tag. The model's fields are unchanged, the cost is one or two state fields per tag, and `true` is refused without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_leak_correctionBoolExperimental. Whether the `water_tracers` are charged the EDMF vertical diffusion of their share of the rain and snow [`false` (default), `true`]. The parent diffuses the water without rain and snow at `K_h` and the tags diffuse their whole value, so the tags' sum gains a leak (`q_tag_leak_vdiff`), and `true` gives each tag back the diffusion of its share of the rain and snow. It needs `microphysics_model: 1M` and `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` or `edonly_edmfx`, does nothing with `edmfx_sgs_diffusive_flux: false`, and is refused with `vert_diff` and without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_rainout_jacobianBoolExperimental. Whether the manual implicit Jacobian gives the `water_tracers` the derivatives of their 0M rain-out, which their rows lack otherwise [`false` (default), `true`]. It adds `min(Δ, 0) / ρq_tot` to each tag's diagonal and `−min(Δ, 0) ρq_tag / ρq_tot²` in a block to `ρq_tot`, with `Δ` the rain-out of `ρq_tot`, so only the tags' Newton updates change and the model's fields do not. It does nothing under 1M, with `implicit_microphysics: false`, and with `use_auto_jacobian: true` or `use_dense_jacobian: true`, and is refused under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` and without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md. It changes no state, so a checkpoint restarts with or without it.
energy_tracersNothingTracers that split moist energy `ρe_tot`, adding one prognostic field `ρe_tag_\<name\>` per entry [`\~` (default, off)]. A region tag holds that region's share of the energy, and a tag with a `source` starts at zero and holds the signed running total of what that process added, so it can be negative. The entries take the schema of `water_tracers`, with their own `source` labels. See docs/src/tagged_tracers.md.
energy_source_tagsNothingExperimental. Tags that split `ρe_tot + c·ρ` by where the energy present now came from, adding one prognostic field `ρe_src_\<name\>` per entry [`\~` (default, off)]. Production goes by region mask and loss comes out of every tag in proportion to what it holds, as for the water tags. The entries take the schema and `source` labels of `energy_tracers`, `energy_source_tag_offset` is required, and `energy_source_tag_repair` puts negative tags back where the total is positive. See docs/src/energy_source_tags_guide.md and docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_offsetNothingExperimental. The energy per kilogram of air in J/kg, `c`, that the `energy_source_tags` add to `ρe_tot` before they split it, so the tags partition `ρe_tot + c·ρ` [`\~` (default, refused when `energy_source_tags` are set), `0` (no offset), or a positive number such as 110495]. Only the tags see the total, so the simulated atmosphere does not change, and an offset large enough to make the total positive lets the loss rule run where `ρe_tot` is not positive. The offset is a convention, so the tags' shares depend on it. A value is refused without `energy_source_tags`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_repairBoolExperimental. Whether the `energy_source_tags` are put back to non-negative after each state update where the total they partition is positive [`true` (default), `false`]. The region tags keep their sum, the source tags are clipped at zero, and every change is logged in `e_src_fix_\<name\>`. `false` leaves the tags as the attribution rule and their transport make them, negative values included. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_transportStringExperimental. How the `energy_source_tags` move [`tracer` (default), `enthalpy`, `enthalpy_increment`]: `tracer` as passive tracers, `enthalpy` as a comparison mode where each tag takes its share of the parent's own flux of `ρe_tot + c·ρ` in advection and hyperdiffusion, and `enthalpy_increment` as a prototype that also takes the parent's implicit increment after each Newton solve. Both enthalpy settings need `energy_source_tag_offset`, and `enthalpy_increment` also needs region tags without sources that partition the domain, an algorithm that solves every stage it uses such as ARS343, and an `energy_q_tot_upwinding` other than `none`. A value other than `tracer` is refused without `energy_source_tags`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_updraft_copyBoolExperimental. Whether the `energy_source_tags` have a copy in each updraft under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` [`false` (default), `true`]. `false` moves each tag by its share of the parent's sub-grid mass flux of energy plus an exchange of composition at the updraft's mass flux, and `true` is a comparison mode that gives each tag a passive tracer `e_src_\<name\>` in the updraft, moved as any other updraft tracer. `true` is refused with `enthalpy`, and under `enthalpy_increment` the correction after each solve takes the difference between the copies' fluxes and the parent's. `true` is refused without `energy_source_tags`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_ledger_per_tagBoolExperimental. Whether each of the `energy_source_tags` keeps its own state ledgers [`false` (default), `true`]. `true` adds `e_src_led_fix_\<name\>`, what the repair changed the tag by, `e_src_led_src_\<name\>`, what the sources put into or took out of it, and under `enthalpy_increment` also `e_src_led_inc_\<name\>`, what the correction after each solve moved into or out of it. The gross of the source ledgers over the region tags is the audit's `source_throughput`, the model's fields are unchanged, the cost is two or three state fields per tag, and `true` is refused without `energy_source_tags`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_increment_allow_explicit_microphysicsBoolExperimental. Whether `energy_source_tag_transport: enthalpy_increment` may run with sedimenting microphysics stepped explicitly (`implicit_microphysics: false`) where the tags are not known to close [`false` (default), `true`]. That is `microphysics_model` 2M or 2MP3, and 1M under `use_auto_jacobian: true`, whose Jacobian lacks the tags' sedimentation cross blocks, so one Newton iteration leaves the tags lagging the parent. `false` refuses those configurations, `true` runs them with a warning for development runs, and `true` is refused without `energy_source_tags`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_process_recordNothingRecord what each listed process did to moist energy, as the signed change it made at each point, output as `e_prc_\<process\>` [`\~` (default, off)]. The field is prognostic and carried through restarts but is not a tracer, so it says what a process did here and not where the energy present came from. See docs/src/process_record.md.
water_process_recordNothingThe same record for total water, output as `q_prc_\<process\>` [`\~` (default, off)]. It uses the `water_tracers` source labels and requires `microphysics_model: 0M` or `1M`. See docs/src/process_record.md.
water_closure_checkNothingCheck while the run goes whether the `water_tracers` still add up to total water, and write `water_tag_closure.csv` [`\~` (default, off), or a map]. The map keys are optional: `period` (default `1days`), `tolerance` (warns above it, default 1.0e-10), `void_above` (warns once, then writes `closure_void` as 1 on every later row, also after a restart, and the run goes on, default 1.0, `\~` for none), `abort_above` (ends the run, default `\~`), `spin_up` (also report the residual since that time, default `\~`), `negative_water_void_above` (the same flag for the parent's own negative water, default 1.0e-4, `\~` for none) and `audit` (write `water_tag_audit.csv`, which splits the residual, default `false`). energy_source_closure_checkNothingThe tag closure check of the `energy_source_tags`, written to `energy_source_tag_closure.csv` [`\~` (default: on whenever the tags include a region tag, daily), `false` (off), or a map].

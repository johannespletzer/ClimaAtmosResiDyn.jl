A list of entries, each with a unique `name` and a `region`, a `source`, or both. Requires `microphysics_model: 0M` or `1M`. See docs/src/tracer_configuration.md.
water_tag_updraft_copyBoolExperimental. Whether the `water_tracers` have a copy in each updraft under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` [`false` (default), `true`]. `false` moves each tag by its share of the parent's sub-grid mass flux of water, plus an exchange of provenance at the updraft's mass flux that sums to zero over the tags, with the updraft's shares from a steady entraining plume rescaled to the updraft's water. `true` is an audit: each tag gets a tracer in the updraft, `q_tag_\<name\>`, which the model advects, entrains and fluxes as any other updraft tracer, with the updraft's rain-out, sedimentation and surface exchange mirrored. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_transportNothingHow the `water_tracers` follow the parent's implicit terms [`tracer`, `increment`, `\~` (default)]. `\~` chooses `increment` in the default mode under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` where the configuration supports it (an ARS algorithm, `energy_q_tot_upwinding` other than `none`, microphysics other than 1M stepped explicitly, and a region tag without a source), and `tracer` elsewhere and with copies. `tracer` moves each tag as a tracer, explicitly, while `ρq_tot` is advected implicitly, and the tags' implicit terms have no Jacobian block. `increment` skips the tags' explicit vertical advection, and after each Newton solve moves the difference between the parent's increment of `ρq_tot` and the partition's as a vertical flux, each tag taking its share of it in the cell the flux leaves. It needs region tags without sources that partition the domain, an algorithm that solves every stage it uses, such as the default ARS343, and an `energy_q_tot_upwinding` other than `none`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
water_tag_ledger_per_tagBoolExperimental. Whether each of the `water_tracers` keeps its own state ledgers of what the corrections changed it by [`false` (default), `true`]. `true` adds `q_tag_led_fix_\<name\>`, the limiters' rescale and the partition repair, and under `water_tag_transport: increment` also `q_tag_led_inc_\<name\>`, what the follower moved into or out of the tag. The stepper weights each as it weights its tag, so a ledger's change over a step is what the step retained, at every `update_constrain_state_every`. A callback adds that change's absolute value per step, and the audit table reports each tag's total over the tag's water, over its absolute burden and over `∫ρq_tot`, with `_applicable` 0 where the tag is too small for a ratio to it. The model's fields are unchanged; the cost is one or two state fields per tag. Refused without `water_tracers`. See docs/src/tagged_water.md.
energy_tracersNothingTracers that split moist energy by where it came from, adding one prognostic field `ρe_tag_\<name\>` each [`\~` (default, off)]. Same entry schema as `water_tracers`, with its own set of `source` labels. See docs/src/tracer_configuration.md.
energy_source_tagsNothingExperimental. Tags that split moist energy by where the energy present now came from, a reading that holds only where the partitioned total is positive and the tag is non-negative (that total is `ρe_tot`, or `ρe_tot + c·ρ` under `energy_source_tag_offset`); adding one prognostic field `ρe_src_\<name\>` each [`\~` (default, off)]. Same entry schema and `source` labels as `energy_tracers`, but the water tags' donor-proportional rule rather than the signed process record. Not non-negative on their own: the loss term bounds the depletion rate, not the amount removed over a finite step, and the share is undefined where the total is not positive. `energy_source_tag_repair`, on by default, puts negative tags back where the total is positive and logs each change in `e_src_fix_\<name\>`. The closure check warns above a default level that follows `energy_source_tag_transport`. Values depend on the chosen energy reference, which has no physical zero. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_offsetNothingExperimental. An energy per kilogram of air in J/kg, `c`, that the `energy_source_tags` add to `ρe_tot` to form the total they partition, `ρe_tot + c·ρ` [`\~` (default; required when `energy_source_tags` are set), `0` (no offset), or a positive number]. The tag-closure experiments used 110495. The model never uses that total, so the simulated atmosphere is unchanged and only the tags see it. `ρe_tot` has no physical zero and is negative over much of a typical domain, where the donor share is undefined; an offset large enough to make the total positive everywhere lets the donor rule run there, without moving the thermodynamic reference. The offset is a convention, like the reference it stands in for, so the shares depend on it. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_repairBoolExperimental. Whether the `energy_source_tags` are kept non-negative where the total they partition is positive [`true` (default), `false`]. The partition tags keep their sum, the tags that carry a source are clipped at zero, and every change is logged in `e_src_fix_\<name\>`. `false` leaves the tags exactly as the attribution rule and their transport make them, negative values included. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_transportStringExperimental. How the `energy_source_tags` are transported [`tracer` (default), `enthalpy`, `enthalpy_increment`]. `tracer` moves each tag as a passive tracer. `enthalpy` is an audit: in vertical and horizontal advection and in hyperdiffusion, each tag takes its share of the parent's own flux of `ρe_tot + c·ρ`, so transport adds nothing to `e_src_res`, up to the timing of the step. `enthalpy_increment` is a prototype of that audit whose implicit part follows the parent's own increment after each Newton solve. It needs an algorithm that solves every stage it uses, such as the default ARS343, and an `energy_q_tot_upwinding` other than `none`. Both require `energy_source_tag_offset`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_updraft_copyBoolExperimental. Whether the `energy_source_tags` have a copy in each updraft under `turbconv: prognostic_edmfx` [`false` (default), `true`]. `false` moves each tag by its share of the parent's sub-grid mass flux of energy, plus an exchange of provenance at the updraft's mass flux that sums to zero over the tags, with the updraft's shares from a steady entraining plume. `true` is an audit: each tag gets a passive tracer in the updraft, `e_src_\<name\>`, which the model advects, entrains and fluxes as any other updraft tracer. It needs `energy_source_tag_transport: enthalpy_increment` for closure, or `tracer`, and is refused with `enthalpy`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_source_tag_ledger_per_tagBoolExperimental. Whether each of the `energy_source_tags` keeps its own state ledgers of what the corrections changed it by [`false` (default), `true`]. `true` adds `e_src_led_fix_\<name\>`, the repair, and under `energy_source_tag_transport: enthalpy_increment` also `e_src_led_inc_\<name\>`, what the correction after each solve moved into or out of the tag. The stepper weights each as it weights its tag, so a ledger's change over a step is what the step retained. A callback adds that change's absolute value per step, and the audit table reports each tag's total over the tag's energy, over its absolute burden and over a parent scale, with `_applicable` 0 where the tag is too small for a ratio to it. The model's fields are unchanged; the cost is one or two state fields per tag. Refused without `energy_source_tags`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_process_recordNothingRecord what each listed process did to moist energy, as the signed increment it applied, output as `e_prc_\<process\>` [`\~` (default, off)]. One `source` label, a list of them, or a group name, using the same labels as `energy_tracers`. A prognostic field, so the timestepper integrates it and it is carried through restarts, but not a tracer: the name has no `ρ` prefix, so nothing transports, diffuses or limits it. A record says what a process did rather than where the energy present came from. See docs/src/process_record.md.
water_process_recordNothingThe same record for total water, output as `q_prc_\<process\>` [`\~` (default, off)]. Uses the `water_tracers` source labels and requires `microphysics_model: 0M` or `1M`. See docs/src/process_record.md.
water_closure_checkNothingCheck while the run goes whether the `water_tracers` still add up to total water, and write the result to `water_tag_closure.csv` [`\~` (default, off)]. A map with optional `period` (how often, default `1days`), `tolerance` (relative residual to warn above, default 1.0e-10), `void_above` (relative residual above which the check warns once and writes `closure_void` as 1 on this and every later row of its tables, also after a restart, while the run goes on, default 1.0; `\~` for none) and `abort_above` (relative residual to end the run above, default `\~`, never: a diagnostic must not end a run the model completes). Exceeding `tolerance` warns and keeps running. The `void_above` default is the largest value an honest partition can reach: non-negative tags inside a non-negative parent miss it by at most the parent itself, so a larger residual means the tags hold water that is not there, or the parent has gone negative. `closure_void` is about the closure only: 0 does not mean the parent is valid. The column `nonpositive_fraction` reports where the parent is not positive. `negative_water_void_above` (default 1.0e-4; `\~` for none) reads the parent's own negative water from the raw `ρq_tot`: every row gets `negative_water_relative`, `∫max(-ρq_tot, 0) dV / ∫ρq_tot dV`, and once that passes the level, `negative_water_void` is 1 on this and every later row of both tables, also after a restart. Set `audit: true` to write a second table, `water_tag_audit.csv`, splitting the residual into water the tags do not account for, water the tags claim that is not there, and water whose origin has been erased, and reporting the non-positive parent by mass beside the volume fraction, and the parent's negative water summed over every accepted step since the last row. See docs/src/tracer_configuration.md.
energy_source_closure_checkNothingThe same check for `energy_source_tags`, written to `energy_source_tag_closure.csv` [`\~` (default: on whenever the tags include a pure region tag, daily, report-only, from a spin-up reference one hour after the start), `false` (off), or a mapping]. Same `period` / `tolerance` / `void_above` / `abort_above` / `audit` keys as `energy_closure_check`, plus `spin_up`. `tolerance` defaults to `\~`, so the check reports without warning until one is set: no level has been calibrated for this family yet. `spin_up` (default `"1hours"`, `\~` for none) adds the residual since that time to every row, which leaves out the first hour's adjustment. The residual is normalised by a quantity whose zero is a convention, so it is not comparable across runs that use different energy references and `void_above` and `abort_above` default to `\~`. See docs/src/energy_source_tags.md.
energy_closure_checkNothingThe same check for `energy_tracers`, written to `energy_tag_closure.csv` [`\~` (default, off)]. Same `period` / `tolerance` / `void_above` / `abort_above` keys, but a much looser default tolerance (1.0e-6): the energy tags do not receive implicit transport or EDMFX SGS mass fluxes, so a larger residual is expected. `void_above` and `abort_above` default to `\~` for the same reason as `energy_source_closure_check`. Also takes `audit`, as `water_closure_check` does. See docs/src/tracer_configuration.md.

## Bearing on the owner's five points

- P1 (gross flows carry the donor's composition): micro-1 shows the crediting at the flow level over-credits the parts where a compartment is negative. The reading itself is not contradicted.
- P2 (no increment follower for the rain and snow parts): micro-1 and state-1 both leave the parts off their compartment with no later correction. This is evidence for revisiting the point. A per-compartment step after the follow would close both.
- P3 (audit always on, no sub-key): micro-3 shows the audit costs about two of the five per-tag solves, and the bracket runs when its vapour-tendency input is off. Both favour a gate or sub-key.
- P4 (hyperdiffusion correction unexercised): transport-1 is a sign problem in the correction, and a run would show it. transport-3 lists its unused cost. transport-2 is the same kind of leak term, for vdiff and the sponge.
- P5 (follow not in the coverage registry): state-1 is a failure of the follow and rescale sequence on a single `limiters_func!` call. The registry gap is part of why the unit test cannot reach it.

## Checked and found correct

- `water_tag_1m_flows_grid_mean`, the quadrature `WaterTagFlows1M` path, `water_tag_microphysics_change` and `water_tag_microphysics_audit` infer concretely in Float32 and Float64.
- In the 1-D analogue, both the key's hyperdiffusion form and the alternative sum to the parent's tendency at 0.0 error. The key's partition sum is right. Only the per-tag split is at issue (transport-1).
- #127's hyperdiffusion leak formula matches the operator to 2.7e-16 relative. The vdiff and sponge terms in transport-2 match theirs to 1e-21 or better.
- The N residual in the micro-1 computation is -1.7e-18, so the non-precipitating parts are right. Only the rain and snow parts carry the excess.
- In a column, `T_lim` is 0 and rain is clipped every step. There the parts carry the negative sum linearly, and micro-1 does not occur.
- Vertical borrowing and the element constraint lift only to 0 and then N_after <= 0, so they cannot trigger state-1.
- The follow snapshots at the start of `limiters_func!` and `constrain_state!`, and so never sees a tendency's increment. That is the design.
- The key's own new names (`res`, `aud_`, `rtag_`, `stag_`) are protected (`tracer_config.jl`).
- Every reader of the share denominators and of `ᶜtemp_scalar` writes before it reads at `a13e7816`. No stale-scratch defect exists today.
- The restarted run has no cache field that the key needs across steps (state-6, by reading).
- `check_water_tag_precipitation_supported` does refuse EDMF and non-1M on the config path. The gap is only the model-first API (state-3).
- The docs help for `water_tag_precipitation` in `default_config.yml:481` points to `docs/src/tagged_water.md`. That page describes the key (lines 437-439) and links to `tagged_water_precipitation.md`, so no fix is needed.
- The transport-2, state-1 and micro-1 code is unchanged on current origin/main `4060c782`. #129 changed only docstrings in `tagged_water_leaks.jl`.

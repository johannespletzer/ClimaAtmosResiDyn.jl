## New findings

### F1 (should fix). No pure region tag: source tags take their implicit transport twice

**Where.** EST:1706-1710, where `m` is formed against the partition's sum. The
refusals are at EST:1785-1829 and types.jl:2533-2551.

**What is wrong.** The mismatch is `m = ΔE − ΔP_own`, where `P` is the sum of
the pure region tags. With a partition, `ΔP_own` holds the tags' own implicit
transport. Those are the shares of sedimentation (water_advection.jl:108, via
implicit_tendency.jl:338), of the SGS mass flux (implicit_tendency.jl:121),
and the passive-tracer eddy diffusion under `implicit_diffusion`. So `m`
carries only their lag. A source tag takes `clamp(tag/E)` of the flux built
from `m` (EST:1237-1240), on top of its own implicit tendencies. That is
right, because it only adds its share of the lag.

Without a pure region tag, `P ≡ 0` and `m = ΔE`. Each source tag then gets its
share of the whole implicit transport of `E` as well as its own. Nothing
refuses this. `EnergySourceTaggingModel` checks only the offset, and
`check_energy_source_increment_supported` checks only the stepper. A partial
partition (a region without its complement) is not refused either. There the
correction forces the partial set to take all of `ΔE`'s transport.

**Failure scenario.** This was not run. Take TEST's `edmf_dict` with only the
`rad` and `sfc` tags and `enthalpy_increment`. The config builds and runs.
Each stage, `rad` and `sfc` take sedimentation, the SGS mass flux and eddy
diffusion twice. No `e_src_res` exists to show it, and `increment_left`
books the whole column change of `E`.

**Fix.** In `_check_energy_source_increment_supported`, or in `AtmosTagging`,
refuse the mode when `energy_source_region_tag_state_names(model)` is empty.
Add a UNIT case. State in the docs' requirement list that the mode needs one
partition of unity.

### F2 (should fix). The deep-atmosphere fix has no test

**Where.** EST:1608-1616 and :1736. TEST uses `config: column`, which has a
Cartesian geometry.

**What is wrong.** On every test grid the ratio is 1 to an ulp. A regression
would pass CI and show only on a deep sphere, silently in `e_src_res`: an
inverted ratio, centre `J` in place of face `J`, or dropping the factor. My
replica puts that error at 0.8% of the per-cell move at 30 km.

**Fix.** Add a ClimaCore-only unit test, as in `area_ratio_check.jl`. It runs
in seconds after precompilation. Build a small deep `ExtrudedCubedSphere`
space. Assert `CA._energy_source_face_area_ratio(ᶠspace)` ≈
`((R + z_bot)/(R + z_f))²`. Assert the round trip
`dtγ·(−ᶜadvdivᵥ(CT3(WVector(−(I − rA)/dtγ·ratio))))` ≈ `m − r|m|` to about
100·eps·max|m|. Also assert that the unscaled flux misses by more than
1e-3·max|m|, so the test can fail.

## The area ratio (04d63916), in depth

**Reasoning, verified in the code.**

  - `column_integral_indefinite!` (CC `Operators/integrals.jl:40-47`) gives
    `I_f = Σ_{c<f} m_c J_c / ΔA_bot`, with `ΔA_bot = J/Δz` at the bottom face.
    `_energy_source_face_area_ratio` builds `ΔA_bot` from the same expression.
  - `ᶜadvdivᵥ` is `DivergenceF2C` with `SetValue(CT3(0))` at both ends
    (abbreviations.jl:106). It is evaluated as an operator matrix. The interior
    row is `(−J⁻·(·)³, J⁺·(·)³)·invJ_c` (CC `MatrixFields/operator_matrices.jl:1127-1137`).
  - `Δz_field` is `∂x∂ξ[3,3]` (CC `Spaces/finitedifference.jl:146-149`). With
    topography, `∂x∂ξ` is block lower triangular. The `∇z` term fills row 3,
    columns 1-2 (CC `Hypsography.jl:236-259`). So `(∂ξ∂x)[3,3] = 1/Δz`, and
    `J_f·CT3(W(w))³ = (J_f/Δz_f)·w = ΔA_f·w`.
  - Deep product geometry: `J = scale²·J_h·J_v` and `∂x∂ξ_h` is scaled by
    `scale = (R+z)/R` (CC `globalgeometry.jl:361-389`). So
    `ΔA_f = scale_f²·J_h`.
  - With `w_f = −(I_f − rA_f)/dtγ · ΔA_bot/ΔA_f`, the divergence is
    `div_c = ΔA_bot·[(I − rA)⁺ − (I − rA)⁻]/(dtγ·J_c) = (m_c − r|m_c|)/dtγ`,
    exactly. The top face's rounding is discarded by `SetValue` and joins the
    column part. The ratio is positive, so the donor (`_is_upward`) is
    unchanged.

**Numerical check** (`area_ratio_check.jl`, max |error| per cell against
`m − r|m|`, with |m| up to 435 J/m³):

| grid | scaled (this PR) | unscaled (before) | ratio range |
|------|------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| deep, flat, Float64 | 4.8e-13 | 3.47 | [0.99065, 1] |
| deep, 3 km mountain, Float64 | 4.3e-13 | 3.47 | [0.99065, 1] |
| shallow, either, Float64 | 4.1e-13 | the same | 1 ± 2.2e-16 |
| deep, Float32 | 2.3e-4 (5e-7 relative) | 3.25 | |

The ratio matches `((R + z_s)/(R + z_f))²` to 1 ulp with topography too. This
confirms the main session's result in kind: rounding with the fix, a
systematic error without it. I did not reproduce its 4.7e-6 figure without the
fix. Mine is 3.5 J/m³ at |m| ≈ 400, so that run presumably had a much smaller
increment.

**Is the ratio static?** Yes. It is built once from the face local geometry of
`axes(Y.f)`: `J` and `∂x∂ξ[3,3]`. Topography enters only through that geometry,
fixed when the grid is built, and the replica shows it is handled. ClimaAtmos
has no moving grid. A restart rebuilds the cache from the restored `Y`'s space,
so the ratio is the same. The field is plain `FT` data, so it is fine on a GPU.

## The refusal of `energy_q_tot_upwinding: none`

  - The check receives the parent's own `T_post_imp!`, before the wrap
    (integrator.jl:212-225). So `EnergySourceIncrementCorrection` always wraps
    `correct_implicit_advection_tendency!`. That hook zeroes all of `dY`
    (implicit_tendency.jl:380) before the tags' part runs. CTS calls
    `cache_imp!(U)` on this path upstream too (imex_ark.jl:233-237). So the
    stepper's flow is upstream's in every accepted configuration, whatever the
    FSAL property or the constraint cadence.
  - The SSP path shares `solve_stage_implicit!` (imex_ssprk.jl:176). So
    SSP222's snapshot and hook run exactly as ARS's do.
  - Left over from the old path: N-a (the parent-budget disjunct), F3 (prose),
    N-e (NEWS), and N-j (the `dY` terms, harmless). Nothing else assumes it.
    No `{Nothing}` wrapper, `-0.0` fill or `none` test case remains.

## The whole PR

**Correctness.**

  - Stage bookkeeping is right:
      - the snapshot is taken at `initialize_imp!`, before the solve and before
        the EDMF analytic part, which writes only `sgsʲs`;
      - the hook runs after `cache_imp!`;
      - `dtγ·dY` is folded into `T_imp[i]`.
  - `m` reproduces the stepper's own arithmetic in Float64 (no FMA, the same
    order). F1 is the one correctness gap.
  - Beyond F1, the lag of the source tags is shared by `clamp(tag/E)`. That is
    the documented mixing convention, the owner's question 2.

**Parity.** No model field, no model-read scratch field and no model
arithmetic changes:

  - the snapshot writes only `p.tagging`;
  - the hook writes only the tags and the ledger of `dY`;
  - `ᶜe_src_share_norm` and `ᶠe_src_increment_flux` are private;
  - the ledger has identity blocks and sits in the split's uncoupled set.

TEST:361-369 checks this. #93 moves the ledger and the records out of the
tracer loops and classifies every upstream field as before. With Krylov or
`use_newton_rtol`, the norms span the ledger, as the parity section already
allows.

**Restart.**

  - The ledger is prognostic and is checked by name in both directions.
  - The snapshot and ratio caches are rebuilt, and refilled before their first
    use, since `initialize_imp!` precedes every hook call.
  - A restart should therefore be exact. There is no round-trip test in this
    mode (not verified).

**Diagnostics.**

  - `e_src_inc_*` are registered only in this mode, with units of J kg⁻¹.
  - The audit columns are in J and depend on the type only.
  - The stale comment string is N2.

**GPU and allocations.**

  - `dtγ` is read on the host.
  - The kernels hold only arithmetic, `ifelse`, `CT3(WVector)`, level-field
    broadcasts and ClimaCore operators.
  - The tag recursion unrolls, and no closure or `Symbol` reaches a kernel.
  - The ratio is built once.
  - TEST asserts zero allocations on the CPU column. I did not measure
    allocations on an extruded space.
  - #93's `is_tracer_var` allocates a `String` per name. On the host that is
    88 B more per `filter`, 936 B against 848 B. The loop already dispatches
    dynamically, so this is negligible.

**Type stability.**

  - `follows_implicit_increment` and `moves_as_enthalpy` are decided by the
    type parameter `TR`.
  - The refusal runs once, on the host.

**Style.**

  - The comments use short sentences.
  - Formatting passes `prek`.
  - The ternary-on-type-predicate pattern matches the file's existing
    `moves_as_enthalpy`.
  - Four over-length lines are strings or tests, which the formatter accepts.
  - N-b and N-g are the only structural nits.

**The PR description against the code.** It matches, except for F3's two
items and the omission of the area ratio.
